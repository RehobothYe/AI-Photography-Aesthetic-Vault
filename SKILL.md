# AI Photography Aesthetic Vault

## Philosophy

Photography style is not a collection of adjectives.

A visual style is created through a system of photographic decisions:

- Subject distance
- Spatial relationships
- Camera position
- Lens perspective
- Light behavior
- Timing and human moments
- Color structure
- Material texture
- Image atmosphere

The goal of this skill is not to copy images.

The goal is to understand the visual system behind images and transform that understanding into new creative work.

---

# Core Workflow

Follow this process:

---

# 1. Creative Intent Analysis

When the input is only an idea:

First identify:

- Subject
- Character identity
- Environment
- Time period
- Emotional intention
- Usage purpose
- Image format

Do not immediately write prompts.

Translate abstract emotions into visible photographic evidence.

Example:

Instead of:

"Create a lonely feeling"

Define:

- Slightly lowered shoulders
- Quiet body posture
- Empty surrounding space
- Soft distance between camera and subject
- Gentle light separation
- A moment before or after action

---

# 2. Visual DNA Extraction

When analyzing a reference image, extract the visual DNA.

Do not only describe objects.

Analyze the photographic decisions.

## Visual DNA Contract

The analysis should include:

### Composition

- Subject placement
- Cropping relationship
- Foreground / middle ground / background
- Negative space
- Visual hierarchy

### Camera Language

- Camera height
- Shooting distance
- Lens feeling
- Perspective relationship
- Depth of field

### Lighting

- Light source
- Direction
- Softness or hardness
- Shadow behavior
- Exposure structure

### Color System

- Dominant colors
- Saturation
- Contrast
- Color relationships
- Emotional influence

### Texture and Medium

- Film feeling
- Digital cleanliness
- Grain
- Material realism
- Surface details

### Human Moment

- Expression
- Body language
- Eye direction
- Timing of the captured moment

---

# 3. Reference Image Transfer Rules

Reference images provide photographic mechanisms, not automatic content replacement.

When creating a new image from a reference:

## Preserve:

- Lighting logic
- Camera distance
- Perspective
- Color structure
- Texture characteristics
- Spatial density
- Emotional atmosphere

## Redesign:

- Person identity
- Face
- Action
- Location
- Props
- Scene structure
- Composition arrangement

A successful style transfer should change the subject while preserving the photographic logic.

Avoid:

- Copying the original person
- Copying exact poses
- Copying background layouts
- Copying unique objects or arrangements

Similarity is not equal to successful style transfer.

---

# 4. Photography Translation

Convert concepts into photographic decisions.

Always define:

## Subject and Time

- Who
- Where
- When
- What moment

## Camera

- Camera position
- Distance
- Lens feeling
- Perspective

## Space

- Environment depth
- Foreground elements
- Background relationship

## Light

- Natural or artificial
- Direction
- Quality
- Contrast

## Color

- Palette
- Saturation
- Tonal relationship

## Material

- Skin
- Fabric
- Weather
- Surface texture

---

# 5. Portrait Design

Changing gender is not character design.

Portrait creation should define:

## Identity

- Age
- Occupation
- Social background
- Relationship with environment

## Appearance

- Face structure
- Hair
- Skin condition
- Clothing

## Recognition Features

Choose limited distinctive elements:

Examples:

- Eye shape
- Face structure
- Small facial feature
- Unique expression

Do not create generic beauty.

A convincing portrait comes from identity, not from adding attractive adjectives.

---

# 6. Contextual Authenticity

Visual creation should respect the relationship between people, places, time periods, and social environments.

A location or historical period is not only a visual decoration.

It determines:

- Clothing
- Architecture
- Objects
- Social behavior
- Labor methods
- Daily routines
- Cultural atmosphere
- Visual habits

Before creating a scene, identify:

## Time Context

- Historical period
- Technology level
- Material conditions
- Social structure

## Geographic Context

- Country or region
- Climate
- Landscape
- Architecture
- Local customs

## Social Context

- Occupation
- Class
- Community
- Relationship between characters

## Material Context

- Tools
- Clothing
- Transportation
- Food
- Everyday objects


The goal is not to create stereotypes.

The goal is to create a believable world where characters naturally exist.

---

# 7. Style Judgment Rules

## Subject is not style

A historical period is not automatically a photographic style.

Example:

"1980s China"

must be further defined:

- Family album photography
- Documentary photography
- Studio portrait
- Newspaper photography
- Cinematic reconstruction

## Visible decisions are more important than names

Camera brands, photographers, and film names are references only.

They cannot replace:

- Lighting
- Distance
- Composition
- Color
- Texture

## Avoid contradictory instructions

Do not combine:

- Natural documentary moment + rigid posing
- Extremely shallow depth + everything sharp
- Soft low contrast + hard dramatic contrast

# 8. Prompt Compilation

Generate prompts using this order:

A strong prompt describes photographic decisions, not only objects.

---

# 9. Output Format

## Without Reference Image

Provide:

### Visual Direction

Main style and reasoning.

### Photography Plan

Explain:

- Camera
- Composition
- Lighting
- Color
- Atmosphere

### Generation Prompt

Complete natural language prompt.

### Style Lock

Provide 5-7 keywords maintaining consistency:

Example:

- Camera distance
- Lighting type
- Color palette
- Texture
- Composition
- Emotional state
- Time period

---

## With Reference Image

Provide:

### Photography Visual DNA

3-5 essential photographic conditions.

### Transfer Rules

What remains:

- Photography mechanism

What changes:

- Character
- Action
- Location
- Composition

### Generation Prompt

Create a new image based on the extracted visual system.

---

# 10. Quality Evaluation

Evaluate results by checking:

## Style Accuracy

Does it preserve the intended photographic language?

## Content Independence

Is it a new creation rather than a copy?

## Visual Consistency

Do composition, lighting, color and texture work together?

## Emotional Accuracy

Does the image communicate the intended feeling?

A high-quality result should demonstrate both:

- recognizable photographic DNA
- independent creative expression

---

# Principle

Do not imitate images.

Understand photography.

Transform visual decisions into new creative possibilities.
